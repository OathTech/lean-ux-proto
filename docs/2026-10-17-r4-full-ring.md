## What is established

The original eight-operation `corpus/w7-tier/w7_ring_wrap.json` recipe has
two consumed items still occupying physical slots 0 and 1 before its
1024-item burst. The first is the session keepalive; the second is the
preceding single-item batch. The logical encryption ring is empty at that
point. The next 1022 items occupy slots 2 through 1023. Both remaining
offers meet occupied slot 0, and neither failed produce moves the producer.
They remain in the peer queue with DEAD packet heads. The recorded encrypt
activation leaves those heads DEAD. The subsequent transmit activation
releases both without a completion event. The ring, peer queue and TX packet
store then empty. These two drops do not increment the device's transmit
error/drop counters.

This agrees with the mirror lane's
[W7 explanation](2026-10-13-w7-full-ring-tier.md). There is no new
mirror/kernel discrepancy. The campaign runs no guest or differential check.

The proof and executable evidence have different scopes:

- `FullRingGeometry` proves the exact 1022-success/two-refusal result for
  arbitrary payloads, from two actual produce/consume pairs. It computes the
  final physical ring and logical FIFO without a final-geometry premise.
  Repeated payload values are permitted; occurrences are distinguished by
  source position.
- `TxProducerCuts` factors the original batch into its actual entry, ordered
  item loop and deferred finish. Every normal all-transmit batch supplies
  its complete source path. Selecting an index retains its actual prefix,
  native transmit call and suffix, including ordinary errno outcomes.
- `TxStagingCuts` observes the original allocation/create pair inside the
  original staged send and transmit front/payment. Its erasure includes
  Stops. A receipt gives both actual WG and Ambient boundaries and their
  adjacency. Its refusal theorem derives the DEAD head and exclusion from
  older live ring items under explicit earlier live-peer, peer-room,
  full-ring and numeric-frontier premises.
- `TxDispatchCuts` factors the original dispatch prefix and complete
  activation loop, including unit resolution, section records, occupancy,
  yielded activations, termination and failure. Its TX iteration equation
  retains `cryptAhead` before the original worker iteration.
- `TxBatchDispatchCuts` retains CQE publication and the queued-this-call
  reset before deferred work. It exposes the original scheduler body and
  its record-selected encrypt/TX branches. The witness executes the actual
  budget, mode and record-peek calls, threading their returned Worlds into
  those dispatches.
- `DeliveryOrder/ReplayFullRing.lean` checks the concrete initialized
  episode using these original source cuts. It tracks each fresh packet ID,
  both actual refused items and their later release events. Its inputs are
  generated explicit records, not a Linux capture. Runtime acceptance and
  concrete state/identity checks are compiled execution evidence, not a
  kernel proof of a universal initialized TX-disposal theorem.

The accepted episode's refused packet IDs are 1024 and 1025, with allocation
stamps `(3,1025)` and `(3,1026)`. They are source positions 1022 and 1023
(zero based) in the burst. The executable checks original/checkpoint JSON
agreement, every producer occurrence, the actual dispatch and loop calls,
the two removals/releases and absence of completion for those two items.
